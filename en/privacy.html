Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer ("we") of HappyMog Timer (the "App") handles information when you use the App.

1. Accounts and personal information

The App does not require an account. We do not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

2. Information stored on your device

The food items you add (name, date type and date, storage location, quantity, price, notes, and photos), your notification settings, and your monthly records are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our servers.

When you mark an item as eaten or thrown away, its details and photo are deleted and only the monthly counts are kept. Deleting the App deletes all of its data.

3. Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only to take or choose photos of food packages. Date recognition (text recognition) runs on your device using Apple technology, and photos are never uploaded.

4. Notifications

Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No external server is used.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. The App requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to access the advertising identifier (IDFA) through App Tracking Transparency.

To serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, Google may collect: approximate location (estimated from your IP address), information about your device and the App, information about ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information.

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choices at any time in the App under Settings > Ad Privacy Settings.

For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases and payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information. The App only checks on your device whether a purchase has been made.

7. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the advertising service described above, we do not share your information with third parties.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.
