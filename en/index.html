Support

HappyMog Timer helps you record the best-before and use-by dates of your food and drinks, and reminds you before they expire.

FAQ

I'm not getting notifications

Open the Settings app, go to Notifications > HappyMog, and make sure notifications are allowed. You can change the notification time in the app's Settings tab. Notifications may not appear while a Focus is on.

The app can't read the date from my photo

Retake the photo in good light with the date large and centered. Always check the date before saving. You can also enter it manually.

Is there a limit on photo scans?

You get 3 free scans a day. After that, you can watch a short ad for each additional scan. The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase removes all ads and gives you unlimited scans.

My purchase is missing after changing or reinstalling my device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Settings > Premium > Restore Purchase in the app.

How do I delete or transfer my data?

Your food list is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Transferring data to another device is not supported at this time.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In regions where consent is required, "Ad Privacy Settings" appears in the app's Settings tab. You can change your choices there.
